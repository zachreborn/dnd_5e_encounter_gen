Open game content
=================
This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 (“SRD 5.2.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Adaptations: monster stat blocks are reduced to encounter-selection metadata,
names/types/sizes are normalized for structured data, and higher in-lair
experience rewards are represented as explicitly tagged variants. The encounter
budget table is transcribed from page 202. No complete stat blocks are included.

The bundled catalog covers the open SRD, not the entire commercial Monster Manual.
Example homebrew data in examples/homebrew.json is dedicated under CC0-1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
